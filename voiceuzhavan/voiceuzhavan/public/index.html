About VoiceUzhavan

VoiceUzhavan lets farmers speak a question and hear an answer in Tamil, Hindi, Malayalam, Telugu or English.

Voice input and output use your browser. AI answers come from a server that keeps the API key private. Soil readings come from an ESP32 when connected; until then the dashboard shows clearly marked simulated numbers.

It gives guidance, not a guarantee. For pesticide doses and serious crop loss, ask your local agriculture officer.
